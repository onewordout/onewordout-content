How the game works

  • Everyone passes one phone around. Each player holds it to see their own card.
  • The crew all see the same secret word. The imposter sees a hint, a similar word, or nothing at all, depending on the mode.
  • Then you go round the table saying one word each, and work out who is faking.

Common questions

Does it need the internet? No. Every round works offline. The app only goes online to pick up new words in the background, and it plays fine if it never does.

How many words are there? 1,500 words in each of the eighteen languages, across 25 categories.

Can I use my own words? Yes — create a custom pack and the game will build hints for it from your words plus the words it already knows.

Do you collect any data? No. See the privacy policy.
